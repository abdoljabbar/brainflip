Brainflip: Tricky Brain Puzzle

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 · Version 1.0

Brainflip is an indie puzzle game built to be played offline and enjoyed without invasive tracking. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses and how your privacy is protected.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. Brainflip v1.0 does not require an account, login, email address, or internet connection to play, and contains no third-party advertising or analytics tracking SDKs.

2. Local Save Data

Your game progress (unlocked levels, discovered endings, sound preference, and hint count) is saved locally on your device only using on-device storage. You can erase this data at any time from Settings → Reset Progress inside the app or by uninstalling the app.

3. Device Sensors & Hardware Events

Brainflip uses on-device sensors and system events strictly in real time to power puzzle gameplay:

  • Motion & Orientation sensors (accelerometer/gyroscope) to detect shaking, tilting, rotating, or flipping your phone.
  • Charging state & volume-down key to trigger specific interactive puzzle solutions.
  • Local device clock/date for calendar-based puzzles.

Sensor readings are processed transiently in memory on your device and are never recorded, stored, or sent off your device.

4. External Links & Google Play

If you tap Rate & Ask for Chapter 2, Share, or Privacy Policy, your device opens Google Play, your native share sheet, or your web browser. Any interaction with Google Play is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. Children’s Privacy

Brainflip does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

If future updates (such as Chapter 2) introduce optional features like analytics, ads, or cloud saves, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety section will be updated before those features go live.

7. Contact

Questions or feedback about Brainflip? Contact the developer via the support email listed on our Google Play Store page.
